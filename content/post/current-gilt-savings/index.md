With Bank Rate at 3.75% and the latest 3-month Treasury bill tender
clearing at 3.97%, refinancing all 28 qualifying gilt issues onto rolling
[3-month bills][1] would save an estimated

## £4.31bn

of interest over the next year.

The ten biggest prizes below account for **£2.26bn** of that.

### Assumptions

- Bill yields are the average accepted yields on the latest 3- and
  6-month tenders.
- The 3v6 forward is implied from the 3- and 6-month spot yields using
  simple interest on an actual/365 day count.
- The 6v9 and 9v12 quarters extrapolate the last observed step of the
  forward curve at a halving pace.
- The rolling annual yield of 4.43% compounds the four quarterly rates
  over 91.25-day quarters.
- The cut off for qualification is 25 basis points above the rolling
  annual yield.
- Every qualifying issue is assumed to pay a full year of coupons.
